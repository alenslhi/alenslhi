### About Me
I am an Information Systems student at Universitas Tadulako (UNTAD), currently in my sixth semester with a focus on E-Government and Public Information Systems. I am passionate about software development, aiming to build modern, minimalist, and highly functional digital experiences. Currently, I am actively developing my personal portfolio brand, RVPS, and contributing to the development of the UKKOM FATEK UNTAD website.

- **Current Focus:** Full-stack development with Laravel, Node.js, React, and MySQL, alongside mobile development using Dart.
- **Design Philosophy:** I specialize in modern UI trends like Glassmorphism and Bento UI, utilizing Figma and Tailwind CSS.
- **Other Interests:** Beyond coding, I engage in digital content creation, including graphic design, photography, and videography, using Canva and CapCut.
